Privacy Policy

Mumbai Local Straphanger · Version 1.2 · Last updated: 11 October 2026

Mumbai Local Straphanger ("the game") is made by Sijo Joseph. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

The short version

The game does not need an account and works offline. Your progress stays on your device. The only time anything is shared is when you choose to watch an optional ad for a second chance in the Android or iOS app: Google AdMob then processes some device information to show that ad.

What is stored, and where

  • Game progress (high score, coins, unlocked characters, missions and settings) is saved only on your device. It never leaves your device.
  • Deleting the app, or using Settings → Reset progress, permanently erases this data.

Optional rewarded ads (Android and iOS apps only)

  • After a fall you can choose to watch a short ad to keep riding. Ads are never shown unless you tap that button. The web version has no ads.
  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect device information such as your advertising ID, IP address, device model and approximate location (derived from IP), and information about ad interactions.
  • Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • On iPhone, you are asked whether apps may track you (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, you still get ads, just not personalised ones.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Google → Ads).
  • How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

What we do not do

  • We do not run our own servers and never receive your data.
  • No analytics SDKs are included.
  • No contacts, camera, microphone or photos are accessed.

Sharing your score

If you tap Share score, your device's own share sheet opens with a short text message (and, on some devices, a score image). You choose where to send it. The game does not see or keep what you share.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If a future version adds features such as online leaderboards, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
